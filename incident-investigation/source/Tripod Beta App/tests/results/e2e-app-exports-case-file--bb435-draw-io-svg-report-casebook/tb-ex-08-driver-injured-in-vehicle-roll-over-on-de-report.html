1. Summary

Five trios describe the incident, from the need to transport goods to the permanent disability of the driver. Seven barriers failed. The Tripod causation paths point to weaknesses in journey management, driver fitness and rest control, driver training, seat-belt enforcement and emergency response staffing.

5 trio(s) · 7 failed, 0 missing/inadequate and 0 effective barrier(s) · 10 underlying cause(s) · 17 recommended action(s).

2. Description of the incident

A driver delivering goods to a remote location left the Area Production Centre on schedule at 08:00 and was due back at 12:30. His failure to return was not reported until 15:00. A search along his Journey Management route started at 16:30 without success; he was found off the designated route at 18:30. The vehicle had left the road and rolled over. Local villagers had pulled the driver from the vehicle while he waited for rescue; doctors believe this contributed to a spinal injury that will probably cause permanent disability.

7. Why it happened: Tripod causation paths

7.1 Failed barrier: Combining load with other loads

Immediate Cause: Journey manager misses opportunity to combine loads (Sub-standard act, Mistake (knowledge-based))

A knowledge-based mistake that has become commonplace.

Precondition: Belief that delivery cannot wait (Organisational)

Underlying Cause: Inadequate communication about urgency of the delivery CO Communication

7.2 Failed barrier: Stop non-rested driver to drive

Immediate Cause: Journey manager does not stop non-rested driver (Sub-standard act, Routine violation)

Precondition: Belief that delivery cannot wait (Organisational)

Underlying Cause: Inadequate communication about urgency of the delivery CO Communication

Precondition: Checking driver on fitness not common practice (Organisational)

Underlying Cause: Fitness of drivers is no issue in journey management procedure PR Procedures

7.3 Failed barrier: Alert and correct steering

Immediate Cause: Driver fades on and off behind the wheel (Sub-standard act, Lapse)

A lapse and possible violation of the "pull off and stop" policy.

Precondition: Driver believes he is rested with few hours pre-trip sleep (Organisational)

Underlying Cause: Resting is not controlled; drivers are on call OR Organisation

Underlying Cause: Too few drivers on this job IG Incompatible Goals

Precondition: Driver does not know of the need for a 20 minute nap (Organisational)

Underlying Cause: Company does not require advanced driving course TR Training

7.4 Failed barrier: Correct reaction on hitting shoulder of road

Immediate Cause: Driver over-steers vehicle (Sub-standard act, Slip)

An instinctive reaction on waking, difficult to resolve by training alone.

Precondition: Driver does not know how to steer out of trouble (Organisational)

Underlying Cause: Company does not require advanced driving course TR Training

7.5 Failed barrier: Use of seatbelt

Immediate Cause: Driver refuses to wear seatbelt (Sub-standard act, Routine violation)

Precondition: Drivers consider use of seatbelts as "childish" (Organisational)

Underlying Cause: Local culture stimulates "macho driving" EC Error-enforcing Conditions

Underlying Cause: Management does not enforce use of seatbelts IG Incompatible Goals

7.6 Failed barrier: Effective search

Immediate Cause: Search team makes detour before finding crashed vehicle (Sub-standard act, Unintentional violation (awareness))

Precondition: Search team not sure about actual incident site (Organisational)

Underlying Cause: Ineffective tracking system for road transport DF Defences

7.7 Failed barrier: Rapid mobilisation of search team

Immediate Cause: Emergency team mobilises after 75 minutes (Sub-standard act, Situational violation (no-can-do))

Journey Management Procedure requires a search within 45 minutes of the overdue alert.

Precondition: Control-room operator charged with outdoor duties (Organisational)

Underlying Cause: Shortage of operators due to cost-cutting drive OR Organisation

Precondition: Emergency room team not on call due to company party (Organisational)

Underlying Cause: Management does not enforce duty procedures IG Incompatible Goals

10. Lessons learned

  • Journey management must cover driver fitness and rest, not only the route.
  • Urgency on transport requisitions should be challenged and loads combined.
  • Seat-belt use is a company rule regardless of local law and must be enforced from induction onwards.
