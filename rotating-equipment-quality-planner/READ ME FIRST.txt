ROTATING EQUIPMENT QUALITY PLANNER - USB / STANDALONE VERSION
================================================================

What is in this folder
  Rotating Equipment Quality Planner.html   the app (single file, works offline, nothing to install)
  Rotating Equipment Quality Plans.xlsx     the register file the app saves your plans into
  Start Quality Planner.cmd                 Windows shortcut that opens the app in your default browser

Copy the whole folder to the USB stick (or any folder). Keep the two files together.

First use
  1. Open the app: double-click "Start Quality Planner.cmd" (Windows) or the .html file. Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. In the amber bar at the top click "Connect Excel register..." and choose "Rotating Equipment Quality Plans.xlsx" from this folder.
     The browser asks once for permission to read and write the file. Allow it.
  3. Build a plan, press "File in register". The row appears in the Plan register tab and the workbook is saved within a second.
     The status pill at the top right shows the file name and the time of the last save.

Next time
  Open the app and click "Reconnect to Rotating Equipment Quality Plans.xlsx" in the amber bar (the browser asks for permission again).
  If the button is not there (for example on a different computer) use "Connect Excel register..." and pick the file.

Sheets in the workbook
  Quality plans    one row per plan (the app reads plans back from here - keep the header row)
  Task schedule    one row per requirement with its frequency band and rank - sort or filter it in Excel; the app rewrites it
  Frequency bands  the standard scale (1 = continuous ... 15 = not defined) used for sorting
  Tags, Register (source), Read me

Rules for the workbook
  - Do not rename the sheet "Quality plans" or change its header row - the app reads plans back by the header names.
  - Close the workbook in Excel before filing plans. If Excel has it open the app cannot write it and shows a warning;
    the plans are kept in the browser and are written the next time a save succeeds.
  - You may add your own sheets; the app keeps them when it saves. Cell formatting inside the app's sheets is regenerated.
  - Site tags must be unique. To change a filed plan, open it from the Plan register tab; the app refuses a second plan with the same tag.

Other browsers (Firefox, Safari)
  Direct file saving is not available. Use "Import from Excel" at start and "Export to Excel" when you finish, then copy the
  exported file over the register file on the USB. Plans are also kept in that browser between sessions.

Data source
  The plans are generated from the Rotating Equipment Maintenance & Inspection Requirement Register (starting point only).
  Standards, intervals and acceptance values are typical industry practice - reconcile them with the site engineering
  specifications, OEM manuals, sparing philosophy, criticality register and regulatory framework before use.
