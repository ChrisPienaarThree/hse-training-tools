    Source material: AI_Workplace_Safety_Research_1.md (this folder). Each simulation lives in simulations/<name>/ with its own README.md, and a data/_TEMPLATE/ folder for adding scenarios — copy the folder, fill in the dataset, add two lines of registration.

    Design rules held across every simulation:

    • Nothing is trained. Parameters and content are pre-authored; the maths that runs on them (softmax, logistic regression, tree traversal, LOPA products, REBA/NIOSH equations, seeded simulation) is real and hand-checkable.
    • Every demo shows a failure mode, not just a success — hallucination, alert fatigue, model drift, under-reporting bias, common-cause failure, matrix banding, narrow-space pattern-matching errors.
    • Independent evidence is marked as such wherever the literature is cited; vendor claims are marked separately.
    • A human stays in the loop in every panel, named for what they verify.
    • Light + dark, offline, projector-friendly — open index.html, no server, no build step.
