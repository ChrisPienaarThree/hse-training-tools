S8 · Dynamic bowtie — barrier status live

Simulation 8 for Integrating AI in Workplace Safety Practices — Day 2, Topic 5. A bowtie built the LOPA way: barriers in series between threats and a top event, and between the top event and the worst consequence. Flip a barrier's status and watch the residual risk move — against what the signed-off, static bowtie still assumes.

Teaching simulation. No model runs. Threat frequencies, each barrier's nominal probability of failure on demand (PFD), its degraded/overdue behaviour, and one common-cause pairing are pre-authored. The PFD for each barrier's current status, the series-product maths, the static-vs-live comparison, the ratio and the risk-matrix placement are computed live — and are checkable by hand.
