S6 · Pose estimation → ergonomic lifting risk

Simulation 6 for Integrating AI in Workplace Safety Practices — Day 2, Topic 4. A camera estimates the worker's posture; a REBA-style score and the NIOSH Lifting Equation turn it into a risk rating — with every step of the sum shown.

Teaching simulation. No pose model runs. The input ranges, the REBA lookup tables and the NIOSH multiplier tables are pre-authored standard rubrics. The joint-angle derivation, every band lookup, the REBA score and the NIOSH lifting index are computed live from the sliders — and are checkable by hand.
