S4 · Four predictive models, one dataset

Simulation 4 for Integrating AI in Workplace Safety Practices — Day 1, Topic 2b. The same structured data scored by a regression, one decision tree, a boosted-stump ensemble and an anomaly detector — to see how each makes its prediction and how explainable each one is.

Teaching simulation. No model was trained. The weights, the tree, the rules and the feature means/SDs are pre-authored. Every prediction, contribution bar and z-score is computed live from the sliders by the standard maths — sigmoid, tree traversal, a sum of rules, z-scores.
Drag any slider to build your own case — all four models update as you go.

Facilitator notes, walkthrough and discussion questions: see README.md in this folder. Add a scenario: copy data/_TEMPLATE/, fill dataset.js, then add a <script> line below and a row in data/manifest.js.
