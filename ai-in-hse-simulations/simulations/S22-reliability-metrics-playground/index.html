S22 · AI reliability metrics playground

Simulation 22 for Integrating AI in Workplace Safety Practices — Day 5, Topic 4. A confusion-matrix sandbox: move the threshold and watch precision trade against recall; change how common the event really is and watch "accuracy" stop meaning anything.

Teaching simulation. No model runs. Population size, default prevalence/separation/drift rate and the framing labels are pre-authored. Every score is a seeded random draw; every confusion-matrix count, every metric, the swept ROC curve and its AUC, and the recalibrated threshold are computed live.
